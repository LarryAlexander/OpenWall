# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in OpenWall a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, respectful feedback, accountability, and focusing on what is best for the community. Harassment, sexualized attention, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that conflict with this standard. Enforcement reports should use a private channel identified in the repository's published community profile. Maintainers must respect the reporter's privacy and safety.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
